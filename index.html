The Studio

Walk in with an idea. Walk out with a body of work.

Amaris Content Studios is Ekpoma’s home for beautiful content: a rental studio where every wall, texture and corner has been styled to look effortless on camera.

Three distinct rooms, flattering light and furniture you will actually want in frame. Whether it is a birthday shoot, a brand campaign or thirty seconds of reel magic, the space does the heavy lifting so you can focus on the shot.

  • Three styled setsMinimalist, Emerald and Ile Oba, each with its own mood.
  • Photo and video readyBuilt for stills, reels and everything in between.
  • Creator firstCome solo, with your photographer or with the whole team.
  • Flexible sessionsSingle, couple and family packages, with time to spare.
